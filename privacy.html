Privacy notice

cDAVF Classifier · Last updated 30 September 2026

Who is responsible

The cDAVF Classifier is a free research tool created and operated by Alex Kostynskyy (the "author"). Privacy contact: [contact email to be added].

What is recorded

  • Anonymous usage records (always): page visits and completed classifications, meaning the options selected, the result, and the classifier and dictionary versions. These records do not include your IP address or browser ID.
  • Only with your consent ("Accept"): your IP address (obtained through the ipify service), a random browser ID stored in your browser, your browser type, language and time zone, and the page that referred you.
  • Feedback (optional): your rating, answers, role and comments. Your email address only if you enter it; your IP address only if you accepted.

Patient information

The tool does not request patient identifiers. Do not enter names, MRNs, dates of birth, exact dates, or other potentially identifying information, including in feedback comments.

Consent and use of information

The information is used to understand how often and by how many people the tool is used, and to validate and improve the classification algorithm. Your IP address and browser ID are recorded only if you accept. Declining does not prevent you from using the classifier.

The information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not intentionally disclosed for unrelated purposes.

Service providers

Some technical information is processed by providers needed to run the tool, under their own privacy policies and possibly outside your country:

  • GitHub hosts the website and receives standard technical information, such as your IP address, when pages load.
  • Google supplies the page fonts and stores the usage records in Google Sheets.
  • ipify returns your IP address to your browser, only if you accept.

Storage and retention

Records are kept in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the author, until the project ends or for five years, whichever comes first, and are then deleted.

Your choices

  • Change your choice at any time with Privacy choices at the bottom of the classifier.
  • For access, correction, deletion or any privacy question, contact [contact email to be added]. Anonymous records cannot be linked to you, so they cannot be retrieved individually.
  • You may also contact the privacy regulator where you live; in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

The tool is intended for healthcare professionals and researchers, not children.
